# Salesforce CRM Implementation

## Project Overview

A self-directed Salesforce CRM implementation project designed to demonstrate practical experience with CRM configuration, data management, opportunity management, reporting, and dashboards.

## Business Objective

The objective was to design and configure a Salesforce CRM solution that provides structured customer and opportunity data and improves visibility into sales pipeline and revenue performance.

## Project Scope

- Business requirements
- Data discovery
- Data mapping
- Salesforce configuration
- Product and Price Book setup
- Account and Contact management
- Opportunity management
- Opportunity Products
- Reports and Dashboards
- Data validation

## Salesforce Objects

- Account
- Contact
- Opportunity
- Product
- Price Book
- Opportunity Product

## Reports & Dashboard

### Opportunities by Stage
Analyses opportunities across different sales stages.

### Won vs Lost
Provides visibility into closed opportunity outcomes.

### Revenue by Product
Analyses opportunity revenue by product.

### Opportunities by Sales Agent
Compares opportunity revenue across sales agents.
