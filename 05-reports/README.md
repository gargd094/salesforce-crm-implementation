# Salesforce Sales Reports

## Objective

Create Salesforce reports that provide visibility into the sales pipeline, opportunity outcomes, product revenue, and sales-agent performance.

## Reports Created

### 1. Opportunities by Stage

**Report Name:** Opportunities by Stage

**Purpose:**  
Shows the distribution of opportunities across different sales pipeline stages.

**Key Insight:**  
Helps identify where opportunities are concentrated in the sales process.

---

### 2. Won vs Lost Opportunities

**Report Name:** Project 01 – Won vs Lost

**Purpose:**  
Compares Closed Won and Closed Lost opportunities.

**Key Insight:**  
Provides visibility into opportunity outcomes and overall sales performance.

---

### 3. Revenue by Product

**Report Name:** Project 01 – Revenue by Product

**Purpose:**  
Shows revenue generated by individual products.

**Measure:**  
Sum of Total Price

**Key Insight:**  
Helps identify products contributing the most revenue.

---

### 4. Opportunities by Sales Agent

**Report Name:** Project 01 – Opportunities by Sales Agent

**Purpose:**  
Compares opportunity value across sales agents.

**Measure:**  
Sum of Opportunity Amount

**Key Insight:**  
Provides visibility into opportunity contribution by sales agent.

## Report Summary

| Report | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Opportunities by Stage | Monitor sales pipeline |
| Won vs Lost | Understand opportunity outcomes |
| Revenue by Product | Analyse product revenue |
| Opportunities by Sales Agent | Compare sales opportunity value |

## Business Value

These reports provide structured visibility into Salesforce sales data and support:

- Sales pipeline monitoring
- Opportunity performance analysis
- Product revenue analysis
- Sales-agent performance comparison
- Management decision-making

## Outcome

A set of Salesforce reports was created to transform CRM data into actionable sales-performance insights and provide the reporting foundation for the Project 01 dashboard.
