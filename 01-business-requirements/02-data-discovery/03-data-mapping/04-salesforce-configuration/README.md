# Salesforce Configuration

## Objective

Configure Salesforce CRM to support customer management, opportunity tracking, product management, sales reporting, and dashboard visibility.

## Salesforce Objects Configured

### Accounts
Configured Accounts to maintain customer and company information.

### Contacts
Configured Contacts to maintain individual customer and stakeholder information and their relationship with Accounts.

### Opportunities
Configured Opportunities to track the sales pipeline, including:

- Opportunity Name
- Account
- Opportunity Owner
- Stage
- Amount
- Probability
- Close Date

### Products and Price Books
Configured Products and Price Books to support product-related sales activities.

### Opportunity Products
Configured Opportunity Products to associate products with Opportunities and track:

- Product
- Quantity
- Sales Price
- Total Price

## Sales Process

The Opportunity Stage field was used to represent the sales process and track opportunities through different stages, including:

- Prospecting
- Qualification
- Needs Analysis
- Value Proposition
- Perception Analysis
- Closed Won
- Closed Lost

## Data Management

The CRM configuration supported:

- Structured customer data
- Opportunity tracking
- Product and sales information
- Relationships between CRM objects
- Data validation and consistency

## Reporting Configuration

Created Salesforce reports to provide visibility into:

- Opportunities by Stage
- Won vs Lost Opportunities
- Opportunities by Sales Agent
- Revenue by Product

## Dashboard Configuration

Created a Salesforce Sales Performance Dashboard combining the reports into a single management view.

The dashboard provides visibility into:

- Sales pipeline by stage
- Won vs Lost opportunities
- Revenue by product
- Opportunity performance by sales agent

## Outcome

The Salesforce configuration created a structured CRM environment for managing sales data and monitoring sales performance through reports and dashboards.
