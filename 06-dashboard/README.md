# Salesforce Sales Performance Dashboard

## Objective

Create a Salesforce dashboard that brings together key sales reports into a single management view.

## Dashboard Name

**Project 01 – Sales Performance Dashboard**

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Opportunities in Stages

**Source Report:** Opportunities by Stage

**Visualization:** Funnel Chart

**Purpose:**  
Shows the distribution of opportunities across the sales pipeline stages.

---

### 2. Won vs Lost

**Source Report:** Project 01 – Won vs Lost

**Visualization:** Donut Chart

**Purpose:**  
Provides a visual comparison of Closed Won and Closed Lost opportunities.

---

### 3. Revenue by Product

**Source Report:** Project 01 – Revenue by Product

**Visualization:** Horizontal Bar Chart

**Measure:**  
Sum of Total Price

**Purpose:**  
Shows revenue generated by individual products.

---

### 4. Opportunities by Sales Agent

**Source Report:** Project 01 – Opportunities by Sales Agent

**Visualization:** Horizontal Bar Chart

**Measure:**  
Sum of Opportunity Amount

**Purpose:**  
Compares opportunity value across sales agents.

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard combines four perspectives:

| Area | Insight |
|---|---|
| Sales Pipeline | Opportunities by stage |
| Opportunity Outcomes | Won vs Lost |
| Product Performance | Revenue by product |
| Sales Performance | Opportunity value by sales agent |

## Business Value

The dashboard provides a centralized view of sales performance and helps users:

- Monitor the sales pipeline
- Understand opportunity outcomes
- Identify higher-performing products
- Compare sales-agent opportunity value
- Support sales-management decisions

## Outcome

The completed dashboard converts Salesforce report data into an easy-to-understand visual management view for monitoring sales performance.
